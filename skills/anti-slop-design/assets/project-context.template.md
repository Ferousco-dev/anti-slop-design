## The product

**One sentence, plain language, no buzzwords.** What it does, for whom.

> Example: "Analyzes Android APKs and reports every permission, library, and asset in under a minute, for security teams reviewing third-party apps."

## Primary user

Who this is built for, specifically. One audience, not three.

> Example: "Application security engineers at mid-size companies who review third-party mobile apps before approval. Technical, time-poor, skeptical of marketing."

## Emotional register — three words

The three adjectives every design decision must serve.

> Example: "Precise. Fast. Unembellished."

## What it must NOT look like

Name the category default to avoid, specifically.

> Example: "Not a generic security-vendor site — no dark hero with a hooded figure, no threat-map globe, no fear-based copy."

## Primary action

The single most important thing a visitor should do.

> Example: "Upload an APK and see a real report — no signup."
